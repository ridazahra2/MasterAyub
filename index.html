About Us

Guided by Master Ayub

Noor-e-Ilm Open School in F6, Islamabad is a labour of love founded and led by Master Ayub — a dedicated educator who believes that every child, regardless of financial circumstance, deserves quality education. Under his wing, hundreds of underprivileged children have discovered the joy of learning, completely free of charge.

We teach literacy, numeracy, sciences, and life skills in an open, welcoming environment where curiosity is celebrated and potential is nurtured every single day.

Our Story

Where It All Began

A simple mat under an open sky. One teacher. A handful of students. That was the beginning of something extraordinary.

Our Story — Coming Soon
"If one child learns to read today, the whole street sees a brighter tomorrow."

Master Ayub began teaching neighbourhood children in F6 with no resources but his knowledge and an unshakeable belief in their potential. Word spread quickly — parents who couldn't afford private tuition began sending their children, trusting his dedication.

Today, Noor-e-Ilm is a growing community. We have a dedicated classroom space, donated books, and a small team of volunteers. But the heart of the school remains the same: free, quality education for every child who walks through our door.

We are now digitising our journey so the world can witness what community-driven education truly looks like. Check back for videos and testimonials from our students and families.
